# E-commerce & Seller Performance Analytics with SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and seller performance using SQL, based on the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

The objective is to identify top-performing sellers, monitor sales trends, analyze customer purchasing behavior, and extract business insights that can support commercial decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The analysis focuses on understanding:

* Seller sales performance
* Sales evolution over time
* Seller performance across product categories
* Customer repeat purchase behavior
* Opportunities for seller development and commercial monitoring

## 📂 Dataset

**Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist**

The dataset contains information related to e-commerce orders, customers, sellers, products, payments, and reviews.

The data was structured as a relational database in order to perform SQL-based analysis across multiple tables.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

* **SQL** — data extraction, transformation and analysis
* **SQLite** — relational database
* **DB Browser for SQLite** — database exploration and query execution
* **Data & Business Analysis** — KPI monitoring and business insights

## 🔍 Methodology

The analysis was conducted using SQL queries to explore relationships between customers, orders, sellers, and products.

Key SQL techniques used include:

* `SELECT`, `WHERE`, `ORDER BY`
* `GROUP BY` and aggregate functions (`COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVG`)
* `HAVING`
* `JOIN` and `LEFT JOIN`
* `CASE WHEN`
* Subqueries
* Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
* Window functions such as `RANK()` and `LAG()`
* Date and time functions using `strftime()`

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Seller Sales Performance

Calculated the total sales amount generated by each seller and identified the highest-performing sellers.

### 2. Seller Performance KPIs

Analyzed seller performance using:

* Sales amount
* Number of orders
* Average basket

The analysis also identified high-volume sellers whose average basket was below the overall average.

### 3. Top-Performing Sellers

Identified the top 10 sellers according to their total sales amount.

### 4. Seller Performance by Product Category

Identified the highest-performing seller within each product category using SQL window functions.

### 5. Customer Repeat Purchase Behavior

Analyzed customers with multiple orders to understand purchasing frequency and identify repeat-customer segments.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trends

Analyzed the evolution of sales over time at a monthly level to identify periods of higher and lower activity.

### 7. Monthly Seller Performance

Tracked seller sales performance over time to identify variations in seller activity and potential areas requiring further investigation.

## 📈 Key Findings

* **Sales concentration:** A limited number of sellers generate a significant share of total sales.
* **Seller performance:** Some high-volume sellers have an average basket below the overall average, highlighting potential opportunities to increase order value.
* **Category leadership:** Seller performance varies across product categories, with some sellers leading specific categories.
* **Customer retention:** Repeat customers exist, but most customers with multiple orders have only two purchases.
* **Sales trends:** Sales increased significantly from 2017 onwards, with several high-performing months in 2018.
* **Seller dynamics:** Monthly analysis reveals significant variations in seller performance over time.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

* **Monitor strategic sellers:** Track the performance of top sellers and identify significant changes in their sales activity.
* **Support growing sellers:** Identify sellers with positive sales dynamics and provide targeted commercial support.
* **Develop low-performing sellers:** Use seller performance KPIs to identify opportunities to improve activity and average order value.
* **Plan for high-demand periods:** Use monthly sales trends to anticipate periods of higher activity and support commercial and operational planning.
